The Story

A town finding its way back.

On August 24, 2026, a fire tore through Old Town Newhall, taking with it businesses, art, and gathering places that this community had built over generations.

Rise From The Ashes follows the owners, artists, and neighbors at the center of that loss — not to dwell on how the fire started, but to capture how a town shows up for itself afterward.

This documentary does not take a position on the cause of the fire. Its purpose is to uplift the people rebuilding Old Town Newhall, not to speculate or assign blame.

Filming is happening through the fall of 2026, with a target completion of mid-November.
