The Story

A town finding its way back.

On August 24, 2026, a fire tore through Old Town Newhall, taking with it businesses, art, and gathering places that this community had built over generations.

Rise From The Ashes follows the owners, artists, and neighbors at the center of that loss. It is about how a town shows up for itself afterward.

Its purpose is to uplift the people rebuilding Old Town Newhall.
